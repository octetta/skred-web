Why Audio is Weird & Hard

  • Hard-Realtime Audio Threads: Zero syscalls, zero malloc/free, zero mutexes, zero GC pauses.
  • Unbounded Latency = Glitches: Even a 1ms hitch causes instant clicks and buffer underruns.
  • BEAM vs Audio: BEAM is built for concurrency and fault tolerance, not microsecond sample render callbacks.
  • The Decoupled Solution: Let C crunch lock-free, zero-alloc DSP math; let the BEAM conduct the orchestra!

Orchestrating from the BEAM

Option 1: Managed Port (open_port)

  • Tied directly to the OTP supervision tree.
  • Simple lifecycle via stdio pipes.
  • Crash together, restart together.

Option 2: UDP Daemon (gen_udp)

  • Detached background daemon process.
  • True crash isolation: restart or hot-reload BEAM nodes without dropping audio buffers!

Bonus: MIDI controllers and hardware inputs can map directly into BEAM actors and fire skode strings in real time.
