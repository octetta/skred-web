Slide 03/11 T+04:30
Why Audio is Weird & Hard
The hard-realtime constraint: zero syscalls, zero allocs in render loop.
  • Hard-Realtime Audio Threads: Zero syscalls, zero malloc/free, zero mutexes, zero GC pauses.
  • Unbounded Latency = Glitches: Even a 1ms hitch causes instant clicks and buffer underruns.
  • BEAM vs Audio: BEAM is built for concurrency and fault tolerance, not microsecond sample render callbacks.
  • The Decoupled Solution: Let C crunch lock-free, zero-alloc DSP math; let the BEAM conduct the orchestra!

Establish mental model: same C DSP core across Web and Native.
