Slide 04/10 T+05:00
Why Audio is Weird & Hard
The hard-realtime constraint: zero syscalls, zero allocs in render loop.
  • Audio render callbacks cannot block: no malloc/free, no mutexes, no sockets/files, no GC pauses.
  • Unbounded latency = immediate buffer underrun clicks.
  • BEAM is great at concurrency & fault tolerance, but wasn't designed for sub-millisecond audio loops.
  • Solution: Decouple DSP math (C) from conductor orchestration (BEAM).
